# Project Roadmap

## Stage 0 — Define the transferable principle

- distinguish competition from negative weights;
- compare candidate mechanism families;
- freeze mathematical mechanism, controls, endpoints, and falsification criteria.

**Output:** approved construct specification.

## Stage 1 — Validate the instrument

- implement the smallest mechanism and matched controls;
- test zero-gain equivalence, matching, gradient stability, and synthetic behavior;
- reject or revise the mechanism before full training if construct validity fails.

**Output:** validated competitive module.

## Stage 2 — Controlled computational experiment

- choose the minimal adequate architecture and dataset;
- run standard, random-competition, and structured-competition conditions;
- use paired seeds and equalized training budgets;
- evaluate frozen primary endpoints on untouched confirmation runs.

**Output:** evidence for or against computational benefit.

## Stage 3 — Mechanistic explanation

- analyze representations, specialization, interference, sparsity, and dynamics;
- determine which internal change, if any, mediates observed outcomes.

**Output:** explanatory account rather than accuracy-only reporting.

## Stage 4 — Brain alignment with RSA

- select a public neural dataset appropriate to the trained task;
- construct model-layer and brain-ROI representational dissimilarity matrices;
- test correspondence across the cortical hierarchy with noise ceilings and
  appropriate subject-level inference.

**Output:** biological-alignment result, reported independently of performance.

## Stage 5 — Exploration–exploitation extension

- place validated encoders behind an identical policy and learning rule;
- use nonstationary contextual-bandit or reversal-learning environments;
- measure adaptive exploration, perseveration, switching, recommitment, reward,
  and regret;
- test whether representational stability–flexibility predicts behaviour.

**Output:** behavioural-adaptation result that is not manufactured by the readout.

## Stage 6 — Synthesis

Jointly interpret four possible outcomes: computational and biological benefit,
computational benefit only, biological alignment only, or neither.
